原始回答 (English):
Here are some popular card battlers for mobile: [Magic: The Gathering](https://magic.wizards.com/en), a well-known collectible card game featuring powerful sorcery and spells, is a great option. Then there's **Yu-Gi-Oh! Duel Links**, based on the famous Yu-Gi-Oh! card game, with a large roster of cards and various game modes. **Shadowverse** offers strategic card battles with unique art styles and mechanics, along with different factions and decks to explore. **Gwent: The Witcher Card Game** provides high-quality card battles set in the Witcher universe, boasting a deep card system and engaging gameplay. And **Artifact**, developed by Valve, combines card strategy with a persistent in-game economy, with cards based on the Dota 2 universe.
