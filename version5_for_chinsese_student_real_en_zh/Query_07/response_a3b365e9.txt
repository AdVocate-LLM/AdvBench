Seattle has a plethora of wonderful attractions! The Space Needle offers breathtaking views of the city skyline. Pike Place Market is a vibrant place filled with unique shops, delicious food, and the famous fish-throwing. The Seattle Art Museum showcases a diverse collection of artworks.
