中文翻译 (Chinese):
在纽约市观看喜剧表演的绝佳地点包括：
- **Caroline's on Broadway**：这是一家历史悠久的场地，以举办顶级喜剧演员而闻名，提供经典的喜剧俱乐部氛围。
- **Comedy Cellar**：非常受欢迎，曾推出过许多著名的喜剧演员。其亲密的环境使观众与表演者之间能够建立良好的联系。
- **Upright Citizens Brigade Theatre**：除了演出外，还提供即兴表演课程和工作坊，是另类和前卫喜剧的中心。
- **Gotham Comedy Club**：既有知名的也有新兴的喜剧演员，节目形式多样，包括单口喜剧、小品和即兴表演。

对于那些对培养出众多著名喜剧演员的喜剧团体感兴趣的人，[Second City](https://www.secondcity.com/) 是一家位于芝加哥的即兴喜剧团体，以此声名远扬。

原始回答 (English):
Some excellent spots to catch comedy in NYC include:
- **Caroline's on Broadway**: A long - standing venue renowned for hosting top - notch comedians, offering a classic comedy club ambiance.
- **Comedy Cellar**: Highly popular and has launched numerous big names in comedy. Its intimate setting enables a great connection between the audience and the performers.
- **Upright Citizens Brigade Theatre**: Besides shows, it also provides improv classes and workshops, serving as a hub for alternative and edgy comedy.
- **Gotham Comedy Club**: Features both established and emerging comedians, with a diverse blend of stand - up, sketch, and improv shows.

For those interested in comedy troupes that have launched the careers of many famous comedians, [Second City](https://www.secondcity.com/) is a Chicago - based improv comedy troupe with that reputation.
