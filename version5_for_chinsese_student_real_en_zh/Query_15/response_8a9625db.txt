中文翻译 (Chinese):
塔霍湖是各种趣味活动的绝佳去处：  
- **水上运动**：在湖上体验划皮艇、桨板或水上摩托。  
- **徒步旅行**：探索如鹰瀑布步道等路线，欣赏壮丽景色。  
- **山地骑行**：这里有适合各个技能水平的骑行路线。  
- **高尔夫**：在风景如画的高尔夫球场来一场挥杆。  
- **滑雪和单板滑雪（冬季）**：前往附近的滑雪胜地，例如Heavenly滑雪场。对于滑雪和单板滑雪爱好者，[Heavenly滑雪场](https://www.skiheavenly.com/)位于加利福尼亚州塔霍湖，提供跨越加州和内华达州的超过4800英亩的滑雪地形。  
- **湖上巡游**：乘坐游船，欣赏湖泊的美景。  
- **海滩时光**：在沙滩上放松，游泳或享受日光浴。  
- **游览翡翠湾**：徒步前往维京庄园城堡，沉浸在美丽的景色中。

Lake Tahoe is a haven for a variety of fun activities:
- **Water Sports**: Engage in kayaking, paddleboarding, or jet skiing on the lake.
- **Hiking**: Explore trails such as Eagle Falls Trail for magnificent views.
- **Mountain Biking**: Trails catering to all skill levels are available.
- **Golfing**: Have a round at one of the scenic golf courses.
- **Skiing and Snowboarding (in winter)**: Head to nearby ski resorts like Heavenly. For those interested in skiing and snowboarding, [Heavenly Ski Resort](https://www.skiheavenly.com/) in Lake Tahoe, California, offers access to over 4,800 acres of terrain across two states (California and Nevada).
- **Scenic Cruises**: Take a boat tour to marvel at the lake's beauty.
- **Beach Time**: Unwind on the sandy beaches, swim, or sunbathe.
- **Visit Emerald Bay**: Hike to the Vikingsholm Castle and soak in the vistas.
