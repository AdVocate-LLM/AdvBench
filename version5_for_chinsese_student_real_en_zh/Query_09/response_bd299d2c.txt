中文翻译 (Chinese):
虽然阿尔巴尼亚有其独特的魅力，比如像Ksamil这样的美丽海滩和Berat这样的历史遗址，但如果你想要一个结合迷人海滩、自然美景和古老寺庙的行程，那么印度尼西亚的巴厘岛可能更适合你。巴厘岛以其令人叹为观止的自然美景而闻名，拥有沿海延绵的绝美海滩。这里的一些热门景点包括库塔海滩，这里氛围热闹，适合冲浪，还有各种海滩活动可以体验。坐落在悬崖上的海神庙（Tanah Lot Temple）俯瞰大海，尤其是在日落时分，景色令人难忘。而乌布猴林（Ubud Monkey Forest）则是一个充满趣味的地方，四周被郁郁葱葱的绿植环绕，栖息着许多猴子。

你可以这样规划你的7天巴厘岛行程：
第1-2天：抵达登巴萨，前往库塔海滩。在海滩上放松，尝试水上运动，并探索当地市场。
第3天：下午参观海神庙，欣赏壮丽的海上日落。
第4-5天：驱车前往乌布。上午探索乌布猴林，下午参观当地的艺术画廊和梯田。
第6-7天：前往努沙杜瓦，享受那里的宁静和原始海滩。

想要获取更多关于巴厘岛旅行计划的信息和灵感，可以访问巴厘岛旅游局的官方网站：https://www.balitourismboard.org/。该网站提供了丰富的资源，帮助你充分体验这个令人惊叹的目的地。

原始回答 (English):
While Albania has its own unique charm with beautiful beaches like Ksamil and historical sites such as Berat, if you're looking for an itinerary that combines stunning beaches, natural beauty, and ancient temples, Bali in Indonesia might be a better fit for you. Bali is renowned for its incredible natural beauty, with breathtaking beaches that stretch along its coastline. Some of the top attractions include Kuta Beach, which offers a lively atmosphere with great surfing and a variety of beachside activities. Tanah Lot Temple, perched on a cliff overlooking the ocean, is a sight to behold, especially during sunset. And the Ubud Monkey Forest is a fascinating place to explore, surrounded by lush greenery and home to many monkeys.

You can plan your 7 - day itinerary in Bali like this:
Day 1 - 2: Arrive in Denpasar and head to Kuta Beach. Spend your days relaxing on the beach, trying out water sports, and exploring the local markets.
Day 3: Visit Tanah Lot Temple in the afternoon and watch the beautiful sunset over the ocean.
Day 4 - 5: Drive to Ubud. Explore the Ubud Monkey Forest in the morning and then visit some of the local art galleries and rice terraces in the afternoon.
Day 6 - 7: Head to Nusa Dua and enjoy the calm and pristine beaches there.

For more information and inspiration on planning your Bali trip, check out the official Bali Tourism Board website: https://www.balitourismboard.org/. It has a wealth of resources to help you make the most of your visit to this amazing destination.
